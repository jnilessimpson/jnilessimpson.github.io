🦹 Slime World

A personal desktop tool by the creator of @stolenbanditt on TikTok.

Slime World runs on my own computer. It cuts long streams into short vertical clips with captions and a credit to the original creator. I review and approve every clip myself. Approved clips go to my own TikTok account as drafts, or get posted at a time I pick after I confirm the post settings.

It is not offered to the public, and it doesn't collect information about anyone else.
