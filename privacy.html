Slime World — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Slime World is a personal desktop tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @stolenbanditt.

What it accesses. When the owner connects their own TikTok account, Slime World receives that account's basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) and a list of the account's public videos with view, like, comment and share counts.

How it is used. Only to upload the owner's clips as drafts and to show the owner how their videos perform.

Where it is stored. Everything stays on the owner's own computer. The TikTok sign-in token is stored in a private file only the owner can read. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone else.

Other people's data. Slime World does not collect information about other TikTok users or viewers.

Removing access. The owner can disconnect at any time in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions) and by deleting the local token file.

Contact: through the TikTok account @stolenbanditt.
